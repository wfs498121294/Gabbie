How to Use Coins

Master the currency of science! Here's everything you need to know about earning and spending Coins.

What are Coins?

Coins are the primary currency in Periodic Table Master. You use them to unlock detailed information about chemical elements and access premium features.

How to Earn Coins

🧠

Daily Quiz Challenge

Test your knowledge! You can earn up to 4 Coins per quiz round based on your score:

  • All 10 Correct +4 Coins
  • 8-9 Correct +3 Coins
  • 6-7 Correct +2 Coins
🛍️

Coin Shop

Need more? Visit the Shop in Settings to purchase Coin packs instantly. Look out for special promotions!

How to Spend Coins

🔓

Unlock Elements

Unlock detailed data for any element on the Periodic Table.

Cost: 10 Coins / Element

Current Balance

Your Coin balance is displayed at the top right corner of every page.
