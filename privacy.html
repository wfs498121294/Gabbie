Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Gabbie ("we," "us," or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we look after your data when you use our mobile application and tells you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

Core Principle: We believe in data minimization. Since Gabbie does not require an account, we collect the absolute minimum amount of information necessary to function.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following data:

  • Device Information: We may collect technical information about your device, including the device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers (UDID/UUID) for the purpose of maintaining your app state and purchases.
  • Usage Data: We collect anonymous data about how you interact with the app, such as features used and time spent, to help us improve user experience.
  • Transaction Data: If you purchase Coins, we process purchase records through Apple's App Store. We do not store your credit card details directly.
  • User Content: The text, images, and journals you create are stored locally on your device. If you use AI features, this data is transiently processed to generate results but is not used to train our public models without your consent.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality.
  • Process your in-app purchases and manage your Coin balance.
  • Generate personalized content via AI features as requested by you.
  • Monitor the usage of the App to detect, prevent, and address technical issues.

4. Data Storage & Security

Local First: Your journals and memoirs are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to your private entries.

Security Measures: We implement appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Third-Party Services

We may employ third-party companies and services to facilitate our App ("Service Providers"), such as:

  • Apple StoreKit: For processing payments.
  • AI Providers: For image and text generation services. Data sent to these providers is anonymized where possible.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our App is not intended for anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with Personal Data, please contact us. If we become aware that we have collected Personal Data from children without verification of parental consent, we take steps to remove that information from our servers.

7. Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have specific rights regarding their personal information:

  • The right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used.
  • The right to request deletion of your personal information.
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising your CCPA rights.
